Cybersecurity Threats

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage or gain unauthorized access to a system. Common types include viruses, trojans, ransomware, and spyware.

Phishing

Phishing attacks trick users into providing sensitive information by pretending to be trusted entities via email, websites, or messages.

Ransomware

Ransomware encrypts a victim’s data and demands payment for its release, causing financial and operational damage.
